Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our process, pricing, or turnaround times? Here are common answers.

How long does a typical service project take?
Turnaround times depend on the scope of the project. Graphic design packages typically take 3-7 business days, website & web app projects take 1-3 weeks, and 3D animation / VFX projects range from 2 to 4 weeks. We provide exact timelines during our kickoff consultation.
Can I request a custom bundle combining multiple services?
Absolutely! Many of our clients combine Interstellar Web + Galaxy Design + Universal Video for a complete brand launch. We offer tailored package discounts when you bundle multiple services.
What assets or files do I receive upon project completion?
You receive 100% full ownership of all final assets. For graphic design, you get vector files (AI, SVG, EPS, PNG). For web/apps, you get full clean source code repositories. For video & animation, you receive master 4K MP4/ProRes renders plus project files if requested.
How do project revisions work?
We include multiple revision rounds (typically 2-3 structured review phases) with every project package to ensure every detail matches your vision perfectly before final sign-off.
Do you provide maintenance and updates for websites and mobile apps?
Yes! We offer ongoing maintenance packages for security updates, performance monitoring, cloud hosting management, and feature additions so your digital products run smoothly 24/7.
